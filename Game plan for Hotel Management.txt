Game plan for Hotel Management System would be: 
1. Rooms would be available for everyone (from start)
2. Customers would select and book any of the rooms
3. Purchasing would involve attaching room_id with cust_id, to reflect rooms booked
4. After every purchase, backend would be refreshed to reflect available rooms & booked 
room 


# Backend 
1. Express routes: would have routes to display all the rooms (available + booked)
2. A booking route: that would attach room_id & cust_id & would add rooms in a booked_rooms db 
3. A customer route: to show their currently booked / owned rooms 
4. Dedicated endpoint that shows only available rooms (for new users)
5. Endpoint to un-book the hotel room

# Database 
1. Would have tables like: { rooms_avail, rooms_booked, all_rooms } 
	so basically a whole new DB for rooms & rooms_booked would have a 1 to 1 relationship 
	with cust_id 
2. Customer DB: would have cust_id, room_booked, room_id, cust_password

# Frontend	
1. Would have a login form and based on the customer's data, I can show him, room_booked & available room but he won't be able to book any of the room because I am restricting room_booking for singular per user 
2. Once customer has unbooked the hotel room and then I can once again allow him to book any of the room
